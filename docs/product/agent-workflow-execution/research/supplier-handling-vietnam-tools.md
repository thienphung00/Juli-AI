# Supplier & inventory management without supplier APIs — what VN sellers' tools actually do

**Headline.** Across all eight tools researched, **no vendor exposes a supplier-side API, and only Odoo (via a paid third-party app) has a supplier login/portal**. A supplier is universally a contact record (name, phone, email, address, debt). The entire industry solves supplier integration with four non-API bridges: **email/print a PO PDF-or-Excel**, **Excel import of lines**, **barcode scan at receiving**, and — uniquely Vietnamese and by far the strongest — **pulling the supplier's e-invoice XML from the tax authority**. Zalo is used by every tool for *customers only*; nobody messages suppliers from inside the tool. `[DOCUMENTED across all 8]`

## Per-tool findings

### KiotViet — the most complete non-API supplier stack
1. **Supplier = contact record**: tên, SĐT (unique), địa chỉ, email, nhóm, chi nhánh, ghi chú + computed "Nợ cần trả". No portal, no supplier API. `[DOC]` [supplier guide](https://www.kiotviet.vn/huong-dan-su-dung-kiotviet/quan-ly-doi-tac/nha-cung-cap/), [payables](https://www.kiotviet.vn/huong-dan-su-dung-kiotviet/huong-dan-nha-cung-cap/quan-ly-no-can-tra-nha-cung-cap/)
2. **PO flow**: Mua hàng → Đặt hàng nhập; add lines by search, **barcode scan, Excel import, or the system's stock/sales-history suggestion**. Two save buttons: `Đặt hàng nhập` or **`Đặt và gửi email`** — the latter opens a compose popup with the supplier's email pre-filled and **the PO attached as an Excel file**. Supplier response is captured *only* by the seller manually receiving. One PO → many phiếu nhập hàng; status auto-moves to **Nhập một phần / Hoàn thành**. `[DOC]` [PO guide](https://www.kiotviet.vn/huong-dan-su-dung-kiotviet/quan-ly-giao-dich/dat-hang-nhap-web-retail/), [email to supplier](https://www.kiotviet.vn/huong-dan-su-dung-kiotviet/huong-dan-nhap-hang/gui-email-cho-nha-cung-cap). Returns: phiếu trả hàng nhập, standalone or pre-filled from a receipt, refund auto-computed. `[DOC]` [returns](https://www.kiotviet.vn/huong-dan-su-dung-kiotviet/huong-dan-tra-hang-nhap/tao-phieu-tra-hang-nhap/)
3. **Universal inputs**: Excel (mã hàng/SL/Serial-IMEI) + barcode at receiving `[DOC]` [receipt guide](https://www.kiotviet.vn/huong-dan-su-dung-kiotviet/huong-dan-nhap-hang/tao-phieu-nhap-hang/). **Killer feature — "Hóa đơn đầu vào": the seller enters their hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn username/password and KiotViet auto-syncs every supplier e-invoice issued against their tax code, then `Lập phiếu → Phiếu nhập hàng` maps supplier + products to KiotViet records and creates the goods receipt.** `[DOC]` [hóa đơn đầu vào](https://www.kiotviet.vn/huong-dan-su-dung-kiotviet/retail-giao-dich/hoa-don-dau-vao/). No OCR.
4. **Reorder**: định mức tồn min/max per item, "Dưới/Vượt định mức tồn" filters + alerts; the PO screen can auto-suggest lines, but no auto-created PO awaiting approval. `[DOC/partial]` [định mức tồn](https://www.kiotviet.vn/wiki-ki-ot-viet/hang-hoa-wiki/dinh-muc-ton/)
5. **Sync**: KiotViet chi nhánh → Shopee/TikTok/Lazada warehouse, real-time decrement on any channel. Receipt→push is `[INFERRED]` (same engine, not stated). [Shopee](https://www.kiotviet.vn/ket-noi-kiotviet-shopee/)
6. **Zalo**: ZNS integration is customer-only, gated to Professional/Premium + verified OA. No supplier flow. `[DOC negative]`
7. **Price**: base ~200–240k đ/tháng; PO/supplier included in retail base.

### Sapo
Supplier = contact record, creatable inline on a PO; no portal/API. PO: Sản phẩm → Đặt hàng nhập, lines via search / quick-create / **barcode** / **Excel batch** (SKU, barcode, qty, price, discount, tax); status starts **Chưa nhập**; converting the PO auto-creates the phiếu nhận + nhập kho. Two return types — **Trả sản phẩm** (already received) vs **Trả lại tiền** (prepaid, not received). Công nợ NCC posts on receipt. **No documented send-to-supplier channel at all** — no email/PDF/Zalo button. `[DOC]` [create PO](https://help.sapo.vn/tao-don-dat-hang-nhap-tren-phan-mem-quan-ly-ban-hang-sapo), [receive](https://help.sapo.vn/thao-tac-nhap-hang-tren-phan-mem-quan-ly-ban-hang-sapo), [Excel](https://support.sapo.vn/nhap-hang-nha-cung-cap-bang-file-excel), [returns](https://support.sapo.vn/tao-don-tra-hang-cho-nha-cung-cap-phan-he-moi). **Reorder is the best of the VN pack**: a *velocity-based* "Báo cáo gợi ý nhập hàng" — "đề xuất số lượng hàng cần nhập dựa trên tốc độ tiêu thụ" — plus a below-minimum report; read-only, no auto-PO. `[DOC]` [kho reports](https://help.sapo.vn/tong-quan-bao-cao-kho), [dưới định mức](https://help.sapo.vn/xem-bao-cao-san-pham-ton-duoi-dinh-muc). Sync: Sapo → sàn for stock and price, real-time. [sync](https://help.sapo.vn/cac-van-de-thuong-gap-khi-dong-bo-kho-va-gia-giua-sapo-va-san-tmdt). Zalo OA/ZNS = customers only. Pricing 170k–999k đ/tháng; supplier/PO not tier-gated on the [price page](https://www.sapo.vn/bang-gia.html).

### Haravan
Supplier record carries **Nợ phải trả** and **Tổng mua**; no portal. PO ("phiếu đặt hàng") explicitly **does not move stock** — "*Khi tạo phiếu đặt, tồn kho của sản phẩm chưa được cập nhật*"; a separate Nhập hàng document receives against it. Lines via search, **Excel ≤500 rows**, or **barcode/SKU scan** (with a +1-per-scan toggle). **Send to supplier is print only** — the printed phiếu is described as "*như một cam kết với NCC*". `[DOC]` [PO](https://help.haravan.com/docs/inventory/stock-process/tao-phieu-dat-hang/), [barcode](https://help.haravan.com/docs/inventory/advanced-mode/quet-ma-vach-barcode-sku-de-them-san-pham-vao-cac-phieu-quan-ly-ton-kho/). Public API exposes purchase orders **read-only** (`GET /com/inventories/purchase_orders.json`, with `received_items`/`not_received_items`) — **no POST/PUT**, so any agent writing POs must go through the UI. `[DOC]` [API](https://docs.haravan.com/docs/omni-apis/inventory-purchase-orders/). No reorder feature in product docs. Sync: configurable frequency + **tỉ lệ phân phối tồn kho** per marketplace. Supplier/PO features start at **Omni Advanced 800k đ/tháng**. [pricing](https://www.haravan.com/pages/pricing)

### Nhanh.vn
Supplier = contact record (code, name, rep, phone, address, email, bank) via [API docs](https://apidocs.nhanh.vn/v3/business/supplier); no portal, no supplier connector. Flow is **receipt-centric, not PO-centric**: phiếu nhập kho with a draft/approval state, printed in two copies for mutual sign-off — no PO-send step found. **Hạn mức tồn kho** (min/max) is bulk-uploadable by Excel but only colours stock green/red — **no alerts, no reorder point, no auto-PO** `[DOC]` [hạn mức](https://pos.manual.nhanh.vn/pos/kho-hang/han-muc-ton-kho). Sync to Shopee/TikTok/Lazada is per-warehouse configurable, incl. **"đẩy ngược tồn"** (push stock up). ZNS is a separate customer product ([zns.nhanh.vn](https://zns.nhanh.vn/)). Supplier management starts at **POS Pro 350k đ/tháng** (absent from Basic 250k). [pricing](https://nhanh.vn/bang-gia-phan-mem)

### Pancake POS
No PO concept at all — only a **Nhập kho** receipt (New → Confirmed → Imported → Cancelled), manual or **Excel template**; supplier optional, scopable to a Bộ phận. No send-to-supplier, no barcode-at-receiving, no e-invoice. `[DOC]` [nhập kho](https://docs.pos.pages.fm/pos/san-pham/co-ban/nhap-kho). Best-in-class **days-of-cover reorder suggestion** from average daily sales, click-through into "Create New Receipt" `[INFERRED, search snippet]`. Marketplace sync is its strength: POS → Shopee/TikTok/Lazada/Tiki, multi-warehouse aggregation, per-SKU exclusion. [link products](https://docs.pos.pages.fm/pos/thiet-lap-shop/kenh-ban/lien-ket-san-pham). Zalo ZNS = customers only.

### MISA (AMIS / eShop / meInvoice)
AMIS Mua Hàng has a real PO lifecycle (request → multi-level approval → PO), receiving by **actual "SL nhận"** against one or several POs, auto-generating the phiếu nhập kho `[DOC]` [help](https://helpact.misa.vn/kb/muahang_theodonhang/); **how the PO reaches the supplier is undocumented**. AI extraction of supplier **quotations from uploaded Excel/Word/PDF** `[DOC]` [AMIS Mua Hàng](https://amis.misa.vn/amis-mua-hang/). **meInvoice INBOT is the reference implementation of the e-invoice bridge**: auto-logs into supplier/provider lookup portals, accepts **XML upload, PDF upload, photo, and email-inbox parsing**, validates against tax-authority data; capped at 20 syncs/day, 60 days history per sync `[DOC]` [INBOT](https://helpv4.meinvoice.vn/kb/inbot-tu-dong-tai-hoa-don-tu-trang-tra-cuu-hoa-don-cua-nha-cung-cap/). ~80k đ/user/month, 10-user minimum; Mua Hàng is a quoted add-on.

### Odoo (+ VN partners)
Only tool with a real **RFQ → Send by Email (PDF) → Confirm Order** loop; vendor reply is still external email, buyer clicks Confirm. Receipt is a separate picking: **over-receipt warns, under-receipt auto-creates a backorder**, plus a return-to-vendor flow. `[DOC]` [RFQ](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/purchase/manage_deals/rfq.html), [barcode receipts](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/barcode/operations/receipts_deliveries.html). **Reordering rules (min/max, Auto vs Manual trigger) auto-generate an RFQ with the vendor pre-filled — auto-proposed, human-confirmed** `[DOC]` [reordering rules](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/replenishment/reordering_rules.html). A true **vendor portal is a paid third-party app** ([Webkul](https://store.webkul.com/Odoo-Vendor-Portal.html)), not core. VN e-invoice is a localization module (`l10n_vn_viin_edi`, Viettel S-Invoice, meInvoice); inbound vendor-bill XML `[INFERRED, unconfirmed]`. Marketplace sync: third-party only. Purchase+Inventory require paid Standard (~$24.90/user/mo).

### Zalo constraint (decisive)
ZNS only reaches **"người dùng cuối đã phát sinh giao dịch"** with the business, needs a verified OA + ZCA, per-template Zalo approval, a ±7-day event window, 7:00–22:00 delivery, and forbids links/phone numbers in the body (CTAs only). Purpose tags are GIAO DỊCH / CSKH / HẬU MÃI — **no partner/supplier category**. `[DOC]` [ZBS approval rules](https://zalo.solutions/news/quy-dinh-chung-khi-kiem-duyet-mau-tin-zns/xdygqtrjjm97k28rsh07wr72), [conditions](https://cnv.vn/dieu-kien-de-gui-thong-bao-zns/). The only free supplier-shaped path is **tin tư vấn**: if the supplier messages the shop's OA first, the shop may reply free for 48h (8 messages) and via API for 7 days `[DOC]` [tin tư vấn](https://oa.zalo.me/home/documents/guides/tin-tu-van). **Conclusion: treat Zalo as a human copy/paste + file-share channel, not a programmable supplier channel.**

## The 3–5 cheapest universal bridges, ranked by traffic captured

1. **Tax-authority e-invoice pull → draft goods receipt.** Seller's own hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn credentials return *every* supplier invoice issued against their MST, structured, legally mandated, zero supplier effort. KiotViet and MISA both ship exactly this. **Highest coverage of real supplier traffic of anything found.** *Agent fit:* pull nightly, emit a pre-mapped draft receipt card ("Supplier X, 12 SKUs, 4.2M đ — confirm?"); write marketplace stock only on confirm.
2. **PO as an emailed PDF/Excel attachment.** KiotViet's `Đặt và gửi email` and Odoo's `Send by Email` are the only documented send-to-supplier mechanisms in the whole set; Haravan degrades to print. *Agent fit:* agent drafts the PO from reorder signals, seller approves, agent emails it — nothing to receive back, so no supplier state to sync.
3. **Excel/CSV import of PO lines and delivery notes.** Universal: KiotViet, Sapo, Haravan (≤500 rows), Nhanh, Pancake all have it. Suppliers already send price lists and phiếu giao hàng as Excel/Zalo files. *Agent fit:* parse the seller-forwarded file into a proposed receipt; the file's arrival is the trigger, the seller's tap is the gate.
4. **Barcode/QR scan at receiving on a phone.** KiotViet, Sapo, Haravan, Odoo all support scanning straight into the receipt; the discrepancy is the scanned-vs-ordered delta. *Agent fit:* the scan session *is* the seller confirmation — safe to push stock the moment the receipt is closed.
5. **Photo/OCR of the paper or PDF invoice.** Only MISA (INBOT) and AMIS quotation-AI do this in-market; it is the fallback for suppliers with no MST-backed invoice. *Agent fit:* lowest confidence — always render as an editable draft, never auto-post.

**What nobody does, so you needn't:** supplier portals, supplier APIs, ZNS-to-supplier, and auto-committed POs. Every tool stops at "propose, seller confirms" — which is exactly the approval-gated shape you already have.
