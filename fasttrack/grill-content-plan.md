# Grill — continuous SEO blog plan for app-juli.com (opened 2026-10-17)

Owner request: open a grill, make a plan, and keep writing Juli blog posts optimised for SEO
following the Semrush blog research; cite sources fully but do not promote competitors; find
competitors like Preny.ai. Answers became **D56** in `DECISIONS.md`; the plan lives in
`fasttrack/content-plan.md`; keywords stay in `fasttrack/keyword-map.md` (D50).

## Goals (minimum requirements)

1. A written content plan: clusters, post list in order, cadence, owner approval flow.
2. A competitor policy: who counts as a competitor (incl. Preny-like AI sales tools), what we
   may say about them, how we link (or not).
3. A citation policy: what must be cited, how, and where.
4. A writing standard every post meets (intent, length, structure, images, facts, Vietnamese).
5. A measurement and refresh loop tied to Search Console.
6. The first posts of the plan written as drafts and passing the landing gates.

## Guidelines

- One question at a time; each comes with a recommended answer and the reason.
- Facts about Juli stay within SPEC; no invented numbers; examples labelled "Ví dụ giả định".
- Keyword choices follow `keyword-map.md` (KD ≤ 30 while the domain is new, one main keyword
  per URL).
- Posts stay `draft` until the owner approves; the owner tags deploys.

## Steps

1. Research in parallel: Preny-like competitors (agent), Semrush blog notes (done, D50).
2. Grill G1–G7 below, one at a time.
3. Record D56 and write `content-plan.md`.
4. Write the first batch of posts with agents (drafts), run gates, merge, hand the owner a SHA.
5. Repeat step 4 per the cadence; review Search Console per D50.

  chatbot / "AI chốt đơn" listicles; content tools named, not linked.
- G2: cite primary sources fully (title + link); competitor pages by name and date, no link.
- G3: diagnosis → optimisation → measurement, but every post framed from a seller pain point;
  plus a track on changes in TMĐT.

- G5: length by intent (no 3,000-word floor), structure in `content-plan.md`.
