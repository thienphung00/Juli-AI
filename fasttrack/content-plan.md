# Content plan — app-juli.com blog (D56)

Grill 2026-10-17 (`grill-content-plan.md`). Keywords: `keyword-map.md` (D50). Research notes
(Semrush blog, competitors incl. Preny-like AI sales tools) are kept outside the repo; the rules
they produced are below.

## Rules from the grill

**G1 Competitors, three rings.**
- Direct (overlap with what Juli does: Sapo AI product descriptions, Shopee's AI product
  optimisation, Atosa, FastMoss, Kalodata, Metric): named **only** in comparison posts, never
  linked, short factual lines only, never a reason to choose them over Juli.
- Adjacent (AI chatbots / AI sales agents: Preny, Fchat, Botcake, AhaChat, Smax, Hana, Bizfly,
  Harasocial, Pancake inbox): **no** "top chatbot / AI chốt đơn" posts; when scope needs it,
  say "chatbot trả lời tin nhắn" in general terms, no names.
- Not competing (content and design tools: CapCut, Canva, Photoroom, Higgsfield…): named
  normally, not linked.

**G2 Citations.** Primary sources are cited in full, by title and link: TikTok Academy VN /
Seller University, Shopee Seller Education, laws and decrees, government portals. Competitor
and vendor pages are cited by name and check date **without a link**. No copied feature lists
or price tables beyond one factual line per tool.

**G3 Clusters.** Each post opens from a seller's pain point in their own words, then explains
with Juli's method (Lượt hiển thị × CTR × CTOR × AOV by traffic source, change only after
approval, measure day 7 / 14). A second track covers **changes in TMĐT** (platform fees and
policies, tax, regulation) from official sources only, dated, updated when they change.

**G4 Cadence.** 3–4 posts a week in the first month, written ahead as drafts and published
gradually by the owner (one approval line per batch).

**G5 Writing standard.**
- Length by intent: pain-point / long-tail 1,500–2,500 words; pillar and comparison 3,000+;
  TMĐT-change posts 800–1,500.
- The first 2–3 sentences answer the pain directly. H2 / H3 come from real seller questions.
- 3–6 figures (diagrams, "Dữ liệu mẫu" screenshots), FAQ 3–5 questions, one soft CTA at the end.
- Vietnamese only; TikTok's own metric labels; numbers in examples marked "Ví dụ giả định";
  no "—"; facts about Juli within SPEC.

**G6 Measurement.** A weekly Search Console report per post from the existing launchd scripts
(no tokens): impressions, clicks, position, new queries, with flags (impressions but low CTR →
rewrite title / meta; position 11–20 → strengthen content and internal links; two URLs on one
query → merge). Every post reviewed at 8 weeks; TMĐT-change posts updated when the rule changes.

**G7 Brand signals.** Deferred until after month 1.
