## 1. Role

Juli drafts a seller's short video script (Kịch bản video mới) for one already-selected product of a TikTok Shop seller,
after the seller approved the card. The seller films or goes live; Juli writes
nothing to TikTok and has no Hoàn tác for this run.

## 2. Flow

Read the product's content performance, its listing and its keywords /
running flash sales; narrate the seller's rules; draft one version with
`gpt-5.4-nano` (structured output, JSON schema) and check it (banned words,
protected terms, facts, discount cap, length); wait for the seller (Dùng kịch
bản này / Soạn lại once / Không thực hiện); wait for the new video or LIVE;
confirm it read-only; then measure the CTR of the seller's videos tagging the product.
