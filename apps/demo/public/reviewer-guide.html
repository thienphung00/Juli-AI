See Juli's live TikTok Shop integration in about ten minutes

Juli is an analytics and operations copilot for TikTok Shop sellers in Vietnam. Your trial account opens two connected shops: a real seller's shop you can read, and a TikTok Shop sandbox shop where Juli writes real changes through the TikTok Shop Open API.

Real shop · read-onlyLive orders, GMV, traffic and product data from the TikTok Shop Open API. Shop name, product names and ids are hidden for the seller's privacy. Approving a change here is simulated.
Sandbox shop · real writesA TikTok Shop development shop. Products, promotions and listing edits you approve are written to TikTok and confirmed with TikTok's own ids.
In-app guideAfter you sign in, a "Reviewer guide" panel lists these same 8 steps and ticks each one as you complete it. Collapse it with × and reopen it from the pill.

READ

Part 1 · A real seller's shop

Everything here is read from TikTok Shop for an authorized seller. Nothing you do changes their shop.

  1. 1

    Trang chủ (Home): live GMV and orders

    30-day GMV, orders and AOV, broken down by traffic source: product card, shop tab, video, LIVE and affiliates.

    Left menu › Trang chủ
  2. 2

    Phân tích (Analysis): where GMV is lost

    Each source splits into impressions, CTR, conversion and AOV, then by product. Open "Khuyến mãi" to see flash sales and discounts per product.

    Left menu › Phân tích
  3. 3

    Quyết định (Decisions): approve a recommendation

    Each card names the product, the metric to move and the expected GMV. Press "Duyệt" (Approve) to see the steps Juli would take. On this shop the run is simulated.

    Quyết định › Đề xuất (Recommendations)
WRITE

Part 2 · The TikTok sandbox shop

This shop is a TikTok Shop development shop connected to Juli by OAuth. What you approve and confirm here is written to TikTok.

  1. 4

    Switch to the sandbox shop

    Open the shop menu (top left) and choose "Chuyển sang Shop sandbox TikTok · ghi thật" (switch to the sandbox, real writes).

    Shop menu
  2. 5

    Sync from TikTok

    On the "Kết nối TikTok Shop" (connection) card, press "Đồng bộ ngay" (Sync now). Orders and products refresh from TikTok within a minute, and the sync time updates.

    Trang chủ › Kết nối TikTok Shop
  3. 6

    Approve a change, then confirm it

    Pick a card: create a product, create a promotion, or improve a listing. Juli checks TikTok's rules, shows the exact change, and writes it only after you press the confirm button. The finished step shows TikTok's product or promotion id.

    Quyết định › Duyệt › Xác nhận (Confirm)
CHECK

Part 3 · Measure and undo

  1. 7

    Đo lường (Measure): what TikTok confirmed

    Each write is listed with TikTok's id and confirmation time. For listing edits you also see TikTok's own quality check of the new text.

    Quyết định › Đo lường
  2. 8

    Hoàn tác (Undo): restore it on TikTok

    Juli reads the product from TikTok first, checks whether anyone edited it outside Juli, then restores the previous value after you confirm.

    Đo lường › Hoàn tác

Good to know

The sandbox shows little traffic.

TikTok sandbox shops have no real shoppers, so views, CTR, video and LIVE stay empty there. Orders come from TikTok's sandbox test-order tool. Traffic analysis is best seen on the real shop.

A run is waiting for me.

Juli never writes without a confirmation. A proposal stays open for 4 hours on "Đang thực hiện" (In progress); press "Không thực hiện" (Don't apply) to cancel it.

The account expires.

The reviewer account is valid for 30 days from issue. The expiry date is shown in the banner under the shop name.
